Measurements are approximate and can vary by jersey style. To check chest fit, wrap a tape measure around the fullest part of your chest. For the closest match, lay a jersey that fits you well flat: measure straight across from one armpit to the other for the width, then from the highest shoulder point beside the collar to the bottom hem for the length. Double the flat width to estimate the jersey chest circumference. If you are between sizes or prefer a relaxed fit, choose the larger size.
